\begin{abstract}
We present Witnora-Core, a certified collapse-path engine that transforms CNF
formulas through deterministic, replayable steps into tractable classes when
possible, reconstructs witnesses, and exports externally verifiable DRAT proofs
for supported UNSAT cases. We demonstrate the system on official CNFgen Tseitin
formulas. Witnora detects hidden XOR structure, applies a deterministic
sparse/min-fill elimination order to control proof size, and generates DRAT
proofs verified by \texttt{drat-trim}. In the recorded v0.9.8 diagnostic,
Witnora externally verifies seven official CNFgen Tseitin rows, including
\texttt{tseitin\_20 c=2}, where the pre-XOR v0.8.4 baseline reported no
collapse on the corresponding Tseitin diagnostic. The method is deterministic
and reproducible from the released source and artifact package. We explicitly
limit the claim to exact-pattern XOR extraction on the recorded Tseitin matrix
and do not claim parity support, industrial benchmark performance, or
competitive SAT solving.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Witnora-Core studies a narrow systems question: can a deterministic,
replayable collapse engine turn selected CNF formulas into certified SAT or
UNSAT results without using an external SAT solver for solving? Earlier
Witnora releases established a collapse-path framework, certificate bundles,
an independent raw checker, public result categories, benchmark artifacts, and
DRAT export for supported UNSAT paths.

The v0.8.4 official CNFgen diagnostic showed a clear gap: the Tseitin rows in
the recorded matrix remained \texttt{NO\_COLLAPSE\_FOUND}. The v0.9 series
added exact CNF XOR extraction and a bounded XOR-to-DRAT proof bridge. The
final v0.9.8 diagnostic records the before/after result: the selected official
Tseitin rows move from no-collapse to externally checked DRAT proofs under the
recorded configuration.

This note is a short systems report. It makes row-level claims about a
reproducible artifact, not broad claims about SAT solving.

\section{Background}

\paragraph{CNF SAT.}
A CNF formula is a conjunction of clauses, where each clause is a disjunction
of literals. A SAT certificate is an assignment satisfying the original
formula. An UNSAT certificate requires evidence that no assignment satisfies
the original formula. Modern SAT solvers are commonly based on CDCL and related
proof-logging infrastructure \cite{marques1999grasp,bayardo1997lookback,moskewicz2001chaff,een2003minisat,biere2009handbook}.

\paragraph{Collapse paths.}
Witnora-Core represents computation as a collapse path: a deterministic
sequence of simplification, decomposition, restricted solving, reconstruction,
and verification steps. A collapse path is accepted only when it can be
replayed and checked against the original input.

\paragraph{Tseitin formulas.}
Tseitin formulas encode parity constraints over graph-like structure into CNF
\cite{tseitin1968}. They are useful here because their algebraic structure is
explicit enough for an exact XOR detector to identify, while still being
invisible to the earlier treewidth-focused route under the recorded diagnostic.
The formulas in this artifact were generated with CNFgen 0.9.5
\cite{cnfgen}.

\paragraph{XOR and affine structure.}
An XOR equation over GF(2) has the form
\[
  x_1 \oplus x_2 \oplus \cdots \oplus x_k = b .
\]
Witnora-Core v0.9.8 detects only exact full-CNF encodings of such equations up
to the configured arity cap. It does not perform heuristic XOR mining. XOR
reasoning and Gaussian elimination are established techniques in SAT systems,
including cryptographic SAT work \cite{soos2009cryptographic}.

\paragraph{DRAT proof checking.}
DRAT proof checking validates clausal proof artifacts
\cite{wetzler2014drattrim,heule2016drat}. Witnora-Core treats native XOR
inconsistency as an internal trace and counts original-CNF UNSAT as externally
certified only when \texttt{proof.drat} is accepted by real \texttt{drat-trim}
against \texttt{original.dimacs}.

\section{Witnora-Core Architecture}

\paragraph{Deterministic collapse grammar.}
The core collapse grammar includes unit propagation, pure literal elimination,
subsumption, blocked clause elimination, component decomposition, bounded
treewidth solving, 2-SAT solving, and exact XOR reasoning for explicitly
supported inputs. Bounded-width and decomposition-based SAT approaches provide
context for this structural route \cite{gottlob2002hypertree,samer2010boundedtreewidth}.

\paragraph{Certificate bundles.}
Witnora certificate bundles store \texttt{original.dimacs}, normalized
formulas, collapse paths, reconstruction traces, result metadata, proof traces,
DRAT status, and hashes. Timing is not part of the certificate hash.

\paragraph{Independent checker.}
The independent raw checker parses raw DIMACS, recomputes supported
transformations from raw clauses, checks SAT assignments against the original
formula, replays native XOR traces, and verifies that public categories match
the proof metadata. It does not replace \texttt{drat-trim} for DRAT proof
validation.

\paragraph{Public result categories.}
Public reports separate logical result, discovery route, and certification
level. The categories used in this note are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{UNSAT\_DRAT\_EXPORTED\_VERIFIED}: \texttt{proof.drat} was
  verified by real \texttt{drat-trim} against \texttt{original.dimacs}.
  \item \texttt{UNSAT\_XOR\_NATIVE\_ONLY}: a native XOR contradiction exists,
  but no external original-CNF DRAT proof is claimed.
  \item \texttt{NO\_COLLAPSE\_FOUND}: Witnora did not find a collapse under
  the recorded grammar and parameters.
\end{itemize}

\section{XOR Collapse Layer}

\paragraph{Exact CNF XOR extraction.}
For variables \(V = \{x_1,\ldots,x_k\}\) and right-hand side \(b\), the full
CNF encoding of an XOR equation contains one clause for each assignment to
\(V\) whose parity is not \(b\). Witnora detects such encodings only when the
exact source clauses are present. Source clauses are retained in the CNF.

\paragraph{Sidecar distinction.}
Sidecar XOR equations define a combined problem: CNF plus XOR sidecar. They
are not claimed to be implied by the original CNF. This note concerns
detected-CNF XOR equations whose source clauses are already in
\texttt{original.dimacs}.

\paragraph{Gaussian elimination.}
Gaussian elimination over GF(2) finds native XOR contradictions when the
detected equations are inconsistent. Free variables are handled
deterministically in SAT cases.

\paragraph{Deterministic sparse/min-fill elimination.}
The sparse/min-fill order is a deterministic heuristic used to reduce peak row
width during XOR elimination. The order and row-operation trace are recorded in
the certificate and independently replayed. It is not claimed to be optimal.

\section{Certification Model}

\paragraph{SAT assignment verification.}
SAT results are certified by reconstructing an assignment and checking it
against the original CNF and any active XOR equations.

\paragraph{Native XOR trace replay.}
A native XOR trace records GF(2) row operations. The independent checker
accepts the native contradiction only when replay reaches \(0 = 1\).

\paragraph{DRAT export.}
For detected-CNF XOR contradictions, Witnora emits a clausal proof guided by
the recorded XOR trace. The proof base is the original CNF, not a sidecar or
modified formula.

\paragraph{\texttt{drat-trim} verification.}
The certification authority for externally checked UNSAT rows is real
\texttt{drat-trim}. Witnora reports \texttt{UNSAT\_DRAT\_EXPORTED\_VERIFIED}
only when \texttt{drat-trim} accepts \texttt{proof.drat} against
\texttt{original.dimacs}.

\section{Theorem and Proof Notes}

\paragraph{Exact XOR pattern soundness.}
If a set of CNF clauses exactly equals the forbidden-assignment CNF encoding of
an XOR equation \(E\), then every assignment satisfying those clauses satisfies
\(E\). Adding \(E\) as an implied constraint while retaining the source clauses
is sound for SAT witnesses and native XOR reasoning.

\paragraph{XOR elimination trace soundness.}
A recorded GF(2) row-operation trace is sound if every row is obtained by
XOR-combining previous or source rows and the final row is \(0 = 1\). This
establishes native XOR inconsistency of the detected equation system.

\paragraph{DRAT certification boundary.}
Native XOR inconsistency is not, by itself, an external proof of original CNF
UNSAT. Witnora counts UNSAT as externally certified only when
\texttt{proof.drat} is verified by real \texttt{drat-trim} against
\texttt{original.dimacs}.

\paragraph{Sparse/min-fill determinism.}
Sparse/min-fill is a deterministic heuristic order recorded in the certificate
and independently replayed. It is not claimed to be optimal.

\section{Limitations and Scope}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Exact XOR patterns only.
  \item Detection cap \(k \leq 6\) in this diagnostic.
  \item Recorded Tseitin diagnostic rows only.
  \item No parity result is claimed.
  \item No industrial benchmark result is claimed.
  \item No SAT solver competitiveness result is claimed.
  \item No full DRAT/RAT/LRAT/FRAT support is claimed.
  \item No general SAT solving claim is made.
  \item The row-width cap is a configurable proof-export safety parameter, not
  a theorem boundary.
  \item Sparse/min-fill is deterministic and useful in this diagnostic, but it
  is not claimed to be optimal.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work and Positioning}

Witnora-Core is positioned among CDCL SAT solving and proof logging, SAT
preprocessing, certified solving, XOR-aware reasoning, bounded-width and
decomposition-based SAT approaches, and crafted benchmark generation.

CDCL SAT solvers combine conflict-driven search with preprocessing and can emit
proof logs for UNSAT instances
\cite{marques1999grasp,bayardo1997lookback,moskewicz2001chaff,een2003minisat,biere2009handbook}.
Witnora is not presented as a competitive SAT solver. It is a certified
collapse-path prototype that emphasizes deterministic structural routes,
replayable certificates, and external proof verification for supported cases.

SAT preprocessing and structural simplification reduce formulas through
transformations such as subsumption, blocked clause elimination, variable
elimination, and decomposition. Witnora records these transformations as
collapse paths and assigns explicit public categories to the result.

XOR reasoning and Gaussian elimination have a long history in SAT systems,
especially for formulas with algebraic structure \cite{soos2009cryptographic}.
Witnora does not claim novelty for XOR reasoning itself and does not implement
broad or heuristic XOR mining. The v0.9.8 route is narrower: it accepts exact
full-CNF XOR encodings and retains the source clauses.

DRAT proof checking and related proof formats provide external UNSAT
certification \cite{wetzler2014drattrim,heule2016drat}. Witnora uses real
\texttt{drat-trim} as the authority for rows counted as externally verified.
Native XOR traces are replayed independently, but they are not treated as
replacements for externally checked clausal proofs.

Bounded-width and decomposition-based methods motivate Witnora's collapse
framework \cite{gottlob2002hypertree,samer2010boundedtreewidth}, while CNFgen
provides crafted benchmark families such as Tseitin formulas for controlled
diagnostics \cite{cnfgen}. The reported result is therefore best read as a
reproducible systems artifact: exact-pattern XOR detection plus deterministic
sparse/min-fill trace recording plus externally checked DRAT proof export for
the recorded official CNFgen Tseitin rows.

\section{Conclusion}

The v0.9.8 artifact establishes a bounded certified systems result: selected
official CNFgen Tseitin diagnostic rows that were previously no-collapse are
externally DRAT-verified after exact CNF XOR extraction and deterministic

cases by public result categories.

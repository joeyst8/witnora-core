## Results — median wall time across three measured repeats (ms)

| Case | Truth | Kissat | W027+Kissat | W030+Kissat | Clauses read by W030 in untimed diagnostic |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `decoy_mixed_256_noise4096` | SAT | 6.891 | 11.477 | 11.551 | 5144/5144 |
| `decoy_partial_256_noise4096` | SAT | 6.657 | 11.296 | 11.045 | 4888/4888 |
| `w030_d0_r0_sat_corefirst` | SAT | 3.091 | 5.565 | 5.681 | 20/20 |
| `w030_d0_r0_unsat_corefirst` | UNSAT | 3.141 | 2.536 | 2.479 | 16/20 |
| `w030_d8192_r0_sat_corefirst` | SAT | 8.319 | 13.874 | 13.975 | 8212/8212 |
| `w030_d8192_r0_sat_corelast` | SAT | 8.499 | 13.948 | 13.995 | 8212/8212 |
| `w030_d8192_r0_unsat_corefirst` | UNSAT | 7.374 | 6.113 | 2.933 | 16/8212 |
| `w030_d8192_r0_unsat_corelast` | UNSAT | 7.498 | 6.145 | 5.811 | 8208/8212 |

On the favorable 8,192-noise **core-first UNSAT** construction, W030 finished in **2.933 ms**, versus **7.374 ms Kissat** (2.51× speedup) and **6.113 ms W027** (2.08× speedup). It generated an independently audited UNSAT certificate after only **16/8,212 source clauses (0.195%)**.

The matched **core-last UNSAT** input retains the exact multiset of clauses and truth label. W030 needed **8,208/8,212 clauses (99.95%)** and finished in **5.811 ms**, versus 7.498 ms Kissat; the source-position benefit was sharply diminished. Thus clause order is a causal experimental factor in this restricted constructed family.

On **all five SAT cases** (including both near-XOR planted SAT decoys), W030 was slower than Kissat; there was no early certificate and all clauses had to be read. On all three UNSAT cases W030 was faster than Kissat. The source-level proof is exact but incomplete for arbitrary UNSAT CNFs.

With equal weight per frozen case, the average of case median total wall times is **6.434 ms Kissat**, **8.869 ms W027**, and **8.434 ms W030**. Therefore W030 remains **31.1% slower than plain Kissat** on this 5-SAT/3-UNSAT pilot mixture, though it improves the old W027 precheck by **4.9%**. Equal-weight average of medians is a descriptive summary, not a pooled independent-sample estimator.

## Illustrative break-even (one paired seed only)

For the 8,192-noise core-first matched SAT/UNSAT pair, the measured SAT penalty is **5.655 ms** and UNSAT saving is **4.441 ms**, giving a hypothetical break-even UNSAT share of **56.0%**. The same pair with core-last ordering gives **5.496 ms** SAT penalty and **1.687 ms** UNSAT saving: **76.5%** hypothetical break-even. Neither has a confidence interval: each uses *one* seed with three repeats. The values do not estimate realistic UNSAT rates.

## Next falsification experiment

Freeze at least 16 independent seeds per noise level and multiple deterministic clause permutations, test on held-out external domains, compare all original/frozen baselines with identical wall/memory caps. Include adversarial misleading near-XOR SAT cases. If the prefix timing advantage disappears once clause order is randomized, report that as a failed generalization, not a proof about complexity.

**Claims excluded:** no universal SAT solver improvement; no asymptotic claim, P=NP proof, or evidence of real-world aggregate superiority.
